Sri Lankan Shadow Education Landscape

This section outlines the macroeconomic rationale driving private tuition in Sri Lanka. Despite constitutional guarantees of free state education, out-of-pocket household spending on private tuition has reached unprecedented levels, creating an immense market for strategic marketing and student acquisition.

🏛️

State Funding Shortfall

Sri Lanka allocates only ~2.12% of GDP to public education, compared to regional peers like Bhutan (6.85%) and Nepal (5.10%). This forces reliance on external tuition.

💰

Household Wallet Share

Private tuition accounts for ~48% of total household education spending, surpassing outlays for uniforms, textbooks, and daily school transport combined.

🎓

Measured Academic Impact

Empirical evaluations reveal an 88.5% pass rate on standardized evaluations for tuition-attending students versus 31.0% for non-attending peers.

Why This Matters for Bunny Pixel's Meeting

Teachers are operating in a highly commercialized, competitive market. Students and parents treat tuition selection as a critical investment decision. Positioning Bunny Pixel as an agency that provides high-converting marketing funnels and modern digital branding directly addresses their primary business bottleneck: consistent batch enrollment and brand authority.
